Privacy Policy

Story Continuity · SPRING DATA LIMITED
Effective 18 November 2026

Your stories stay on your device

Story Continuity stores your projects, characters, facts, scenes, events, node icons/photos and revision history in the app’s local storage. We do not upload this content to our servers or send it to an AI service. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK or tracking.

Optional node photos

You can select a photo with Apple’s system Photos picker or take a photo after granting camera permission. The app receives only the images you choose. It creates a small square JPEG thumbnail without copying the source image’s location or camera metadata. Thumbnails stay in your local project and may be included in project backups, saved revisions and device backups. Original photos are unchanged; camera images are not automatically saved to your Photos library. Remove a node photo through View details → Change icon or photo, or delete its project. Older saved revisions and exported backups may still contain earlier thumbnails.

Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and payments. The app uses Apple StoreKit to obtain product information and verify purchase status, including transaction identifiers, product identifiers and subscription expiry or revocation. Verification happens through Apple’s services and on your device. We do not receive your payment card details, and we do not operate a purchase-validation server. Apple’s own privacy terms govern its processing. Apple may provide the developer with sales, financial and diagnostic information through its developer services.

Backups and exports

You can export a project backup or an editor report to a location you choose. Those files may contain your story content, node thumbnails and revision history (project backups include thumbnails; editor reports contain text). Files saved in iCloud Drive, another cloud provider, or sent to someone else are handled by that provider or recipient. Your device’s system backup may also include the app’s data according to your settings. The app does not provide automatic cross-device synchronisation.

Local technology

The embedded Relate database stores connected story information locally. It does not send your projects to a Relate service. The app uses local preferences to remember the project selected for free editing and the sample project you have opened. File timestamps are accessed only for files the app owns or files you select.

Retention and deletion

Projects remain on the device until you delete them or remove the app. Deleting a project also deletes its local revision history. Exported files and system backups must be managed separately in their storage locations. Cancelling a subscription does not delete your work: saved projects and revisions remain readable and exportable. Apple retains purchase records under its own policies; removing the app does not cancel a subscription.

If you contact us

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you send. We use it to respond to your request and keep correspondence only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Our email provider processes that correspondence for us. Please avoid sending unpublished story content unless necessary. Privacy questions and applicable access or deletion requests can be sent to hello@springdata.uk.

Children and permissions

The app is intended for writers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. It does not request access to contacts, location or microphone. Camera access is optional and requested only when you choose Take photo; the system Photos picker does not require access to your entire photo library. Import and export use the system file picker with files you choose.

Changes

We will update the effective date when this policy changes. If new features change how personal data is handled, we will update this policy and any required disclosures before releasing them.
