Getting started

Create a project or try The Observatory sample. Add scenes, characters and facts, then discovery, telling or use-of-knowledge events. Use lower story-time numbers for earlier events. Scene reading order is independent of story time.

Choose your free editing project

In the library, open a project’s ⋯ menu or hold its card and choose Use for free editing. The selected card shows a checkmark and Free plan project; its menu says Selected for free plan. The choice is remembered when you reopen the app. You can choose one project with up to 50 events and one book. With Pro, all projects remain editable: this selection only determines which eligible project stays editable if Pro ends. It does not cancel or change your subscription.

Choose a graph layout

Open Layout to choose Network, Tree or Circle. Tree puts prerequisites above consequences, retains cross-links and warnings, and supports panning through deeper branches. Its rows show connections rather than story time. Circle places the same items around a ring. Each layout keeps its own manual positions and bent lines until you close the graph. Reset layout affects only the current layout.

Arrange the graph

Tap Arrange to drag nodes or the small connection handles. Nodes move with their lines attached; handles bend individual lines. Drag empty background to pan. Reset layout restores the automatic arrangement, while Reset map resets only zoom and pan. Arrangements last until you close the graph and never change your story or its relationships.

Graph shortcuts

Hold or right-click a graph node, or select it and use the ⋯ menu. Open details, change its icon or photo, focus on its neighbourhood, expand connections or hide it from the current view. Expansion follows the current connection filter. Expand stays visible and explains when all available neighbours are already shown. Choose Focus here, then expand a neighbouring node to explore step by step. On iPhone, the compact selected-node card keeps View details, Expand and the actions menu close at hand; full descriptions and connections are in View details. Hide from view never deletes story content: use Undo or Restore hidden, or close and reopen the graph. Search still finds hidden nodes.

Node details and appearance

Tap a graph node, then View details to read its full record and connections. In an editable saved project, Change icon or photo lets you pick an icon, choose a photo or take a photo on a camera-equipped device. Characters use circles, events diamonds, scenes rounded squares and facts hexagons. Custom artwork keeps the type shape and badge. Photos stay in the local project and its backups/revisions. Use the default icon or remove the photo to reset it; older backups/revisions may still contain earlier photos.

Explore connections

Choose Explore sample graph on the home screen, View graph on a project card, or Graph inside a project. Try a change opens a safe practice copy: move Alice’s discovery to 25 to see the conflict and downstream review, then restore 10. Saved projects stay unchanged. Tap an item for its details, search to find a character or event, or focus on nearby connections. Follow consequences shows dependent events. Drag and zoom the map, or switch to List for full readable labels. Map positions do not represent story time. Relationship names start hidden. Tap Show labels to reveal them, then Hide labels to hide them again; lines and arrows stay visible. Dashed lines connect story details; solid lines connect event dependencies. Dashed does not mean broken. Open Graph key for the full legend. Graph exploration is included in both plans.

Plans and billing

The free plan includes one editable project, one book and up to 50 events, with continuity checks. Pro includes multiple and larger projects, connected books, saved revisions and editor reports. UK prices are £3.99 per month or £24.99 per year. Eligible new subscribers receive a seven-day free trial, followed by the full price of the selected plan. Prices and eligibility are shown in the app and confirmed by Apple. Prices in other territories vary.

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Cancel at least 24 hours before a trial or billing period ends to avoid the next charge. Manage subscriptions in the app’s Settings or in your Apple Account. Deleting the app does not cancel billing.

Restore access

Use Settings → Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Restore Purchases restores subscription access; it does not transfer story files from another device.

Protect and move your work

Open a project → Project actions → Export backup. Save the JSON file somewhere safe. On another device, choose Import backup from the library. Importing a separate copy creates a new project; restoring an existing project asks for confirmation before replacement. Saved work remains readable and exportable after cancellation.

What checks cover

Checks use information and dependencies you enter. They do not automatically read a manuscript or guarantee that a story has no plot holes. Equal or missing times are reported as uncertain. A missing knowledge source is not proof of a contradiction.
